The feature: during fast-forward, melonDS keeps Nintendo DS background music at 1x by letting the ARM7 sound driver keep playing (game-visible state untouched), muting the BGM's SPU channels, and rendering the same sequence on the host from blobs copied out of main RAM, paced by the audio device. Sound effects stay on the emulated clock and are time-stretched to original pitch. Core code: melonDS/src/Sound/SndCmdTracker.{h,cpp} (PXI tag-7 command sniffer and host state machine), BgmRenderer.{h,cpp} (two-voice renderer with crossfade and 1x-rate fader glide, wrapping the vendored SSEQPlayer), SdatIndex (classifier), TimeStretch (WSOLA), hooks in melonDS/src/NDS.cpp, SPU.cpp (Mix, SPUChannel::Start, ReadOutputStretched), frontend audio in melonDS/src/frontend/qt_sdl/EmuInstanceAudio.cpp and android/melonDS-android/app/src/main/cpp/{MelonInstance.cpp,OboeCallback.cpp}. NitroSDK driver source for reference: github.com/ntrtwl/NitroSDK (libraries/snd/src/ARM7/*.c, include/nitro/snd/common/*.h).
